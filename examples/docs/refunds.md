# Refund policy

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five to seven
business days. Orders placed with a gift card receive store credit instead.
To request a refund, open the order in your account and choose the refund
option; our team reviews every request within 24 hours.

Chargebacks filed with your bank pause the refund process until the dispute
is resolved. If your refund has not arrived after seven business days,
contact support with your order number ready.
